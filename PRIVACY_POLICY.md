# LoneFighter — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-15_

LoneFighter is a single-player mobile game. We've built it to collect as little as possible.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** LoneFighter does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or any third party.

Your game progress (unlocked weapons, total kills, longest survival time, settings) is saved **locally on your device** as a single JSON file in the app's sandboxed documents directory. It never leaves the device. Deleting the app removes the save file.

## What we don't do

- We do not use analytics.
- We do not use advertising SDKs.
- We do not use crash reporting SDKs.
- We do not have a backend server.
- We do not show ads.
- We do not track you across apps or websites.
- We do not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or location.

The only system services LoneFighter touches are: the audio output, the display, your touch input, the gamepad if connected, and the iOS haptic engine (if you've enabled vibration in Settings).

## Children's privacy

LoneFighter does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns, contact us at the address below.

## Changes to this policy

If a future update adds any data-collecting feature (e.g. leaderboards, cloud saves, opt-in analytics), we'll update this document and the App Store privacy nutrition label _before_ the feature ships.
